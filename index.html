To my favourite idiot, my biggest headache, and somehow, the person I still haven’t gotten tired of. 😂❤️

From our stupidest jokes that nobody else understands, to those random conversations that started with "one small thing" and somehow lasted for hours… we’ve made so many memories together. Some are so embarrassing that I genuinely hope nobody ever finds out about them.

We’ve laughed until our stomachs hurt, annoyed each other for absolutely no reason, had our little fights, misunderstood each other, and still somehow ended up right back together like nothing happened. And honestly, that’s what makes our friendship so special.

Life has changed so much, and maybe we’ll change too. We’ll get busy, meet new people, have different priorities and make new memories. But I really hope there’s always a little corner of our lives that belongs to these stupid, beautiful memories we made together.

Thank you for being there through the good days, bad days, dramatic days, and especially my “I’m fine” days when we both knew I was absolutely NOT fine. 😂

I don’t know what the future looks like, but I know one thing — I’ll always be grateful that somewhere along the way, life decided to give me you as my best friend. ❤️

Now stop getting emotional after reading this. You’re still annoying. And unfortunately, you’re stuck with me. 🫶🏻😂
